# Ground legal and policy claims in the primary record

What a skill says a law, a judgment, a licence, a standard or a service's policy says is what
the user acts on: whether a text or a translation may be copied, what a public-domain
claim rests on. The skill repeats the claim with the confidence the file gives
it, so an error here costs the user more than any other error in the repository.

- Quote the fact's own record: the judgment's operative part on EUR-Lex, the docket entry,
  the licence text, the standard's own page, the service's own policy page. A blog post, a
  summary or your memory can point you to a source; it cannot stand in for one.
- Tag each claim (GROUNDED / UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTED, or Gap) and date the reading.
  Policies change without notice, and an undated claim cannot be re-checked.
- Keep the file's candour. Where the law is unsettled or unknown, say so: the skill tells
  the user exactly that, and a confident-sounding line here becomes false reassurance there.
